📖 About Cricket

Learn the basics of one of the world's most popular sports.

🏏 What is Cricket?

Cricket is a bat-and-ball team sport. Two teams play against each other, with one team batting and the other team bowling and fielding.

👥 Teams

A traditional cricket team has eleven players. Players may have different responsibilities such as batting, bowling, wicketkeeping and fielding.

🎯 Main Objective

The batting team tries to score runs while the bowling and fielding team tries to restrict runs and dismiss batters.

🌍 Different Formats

International cricket is played in formats including Test cricket, One Day Internationals and Twenty20 cricket.

🧠 Strategy

Cricket requires decision-making, shot selection, bowling plans, field placement and understanding of match situations.

❤️ Spirit of Cricket

Cricket also emphasizes teamwork, discipline, respect for opponents and playing fairly.
